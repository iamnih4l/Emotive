Camera Expression Adaptation

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

EMOTIVE observes subtle facial cues (smiles, brow furrowing, surprise) locally inside your browser via MediaPipe WebAssembly. No video, images, or face biometrics are ever uploaded or saved anywhere.

• Only aggregated emotional labels shape the story prompt.

• Procedural audio ambiance responds harmoniously.

• You can turn the camera off or use manual mood buttons anytime.

Privacy Architecture

1. Client-Side Only Inference

MediaPipe FaceLandmarker runs strictly inside your local browser tab via WebAssembly and WebGL. Video frames are analyzed directly in RAM and immediately discarded.

2. Zero Biometric Storage or Transmission

Neither the Express backend nor the Google Gemini API ever receives video frames, photos, facial coordinates, or landmark arrays.

3. Aggregated Signals Only

When generating a paragraph, only high-level aggregated categories (e.g., "HAPPY" or "FEAR") are bundled into the narrative prompt payload.

4. Instant Hardware Teardown

When you click "Stop Camera", all active MediaStream tracks are explicitly terminated, shutting down camera hardware immediately.
