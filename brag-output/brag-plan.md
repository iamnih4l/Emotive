# Brag Plan: EMOTIVE

## What is this app?
An adaptive literary engine where Google Gemini generates stories one paragraph at a time, dynamically reshaping narrative atmosphere, plot twists, pacing, and procedural ambient soundscapes based on real-time browser facial-expression cues.

## The angle
"What if the book was reading you?"
Traditional books are frozen artifacts, and standard AI story generators dump 1,000 words in one generic shot. EMOTIVE treats reading as a bi-directional performance: every smile, frown, or widened eye reshapes the next paragraph and shifts the procedural musical score in real time.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dramatic obsidian darkness with golden embers. A large typography slam:
"WHAT IF THE BOOK WAS READING YOU?"
followed by the pulsing golden EMOTIVE iris beacon.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Reader Reacts**: A reader's face shows surprise; local MediaPipe FACS blendshapes calculate `SURPRISE: 91%` client-side with 0% data leakage.
- **Live Gemini Branching**: Gemini 2.5 Flash crafts the next paragraph with drop-cap styling, twisting the plot to match the emotion.
- **Procedural Soundscape Shift**: The Web Audio synthesizer smoothly modulates from Cmaj9 to an eerie Am7 chord as the weather in the story turns to storm.

## Outro / punchline
"Every story feels different when it feels you."
The Kurt Vonnegut Emotional Arc trajectory draws across the screen, revealing the reader's emotional journey.
Call to action: "Try it live at emotive-omega.vercel.app | 100% Client-Side Privacy | Open Source".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Select premise ("The Salt Keeper of Eventide") & enable ambient soundscape.
2. **Key Action**: As paragraph 1 reads, the reader gasps in surprise — the local vision HUD triggers `CUE: SURPRISED (91%)` and prompts Gemini to inject a secret subterranean passageway into paragraph 2.
3. **Result**: The story concludes with the interactive Kurt Vonnegut Emotional Arc graph and 1-click share card.
